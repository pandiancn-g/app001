Bayaan Seasonality Pattern Highlighter

A research-grade AI tool that detects, adjusts, explains, and monitors seasonal patterns in Abu Dhabi economic indicators. It prevents wrong conclusions by separating normal calendar effects from real economic momentum.

Simple purpose

If construction output falls 22% in Q3, the tool explains whether this is a normal summer heat slowdown or a genuine sector weakness.

Advanced purpose

It produces raw, seasonally adjusted, trend-cycle, seasonal, calendar, and irregular components with AI explanation, diagnostics, alerts, audit trails, and bilingual reporting.

7. Real-time alert center

Alerts are generated when new data arrives, model diagnostics fail, or seasonal patterns evolve.

Open alerts

High

Spectral test indicates remaining seasonal pattern after adjustment. Re-estimation recommended.

Medium

Construction output below normal summer range

Q3 decline is 30%; historical summer norm is 18% to 25%. Non-seasonal weakness may exist.

Advisory

Retail seasonality shifting from December to November

White Friday and e-commerce growth appear to increase November seasonal factors.

Alert logic

Alert type Trigger Action
Seasonal anomaly Irregular component exceeds threshold, e.g. >2 standard deviations Domain specialist review
Evolving seasonality Seasonal factor changes materially over re-estimation cycles Update model specification
Residual seasonality Adjusted series still shows seasonal pattern Block or flag SA publication
Short history warning Series lacks enough observations for robust X-13 Use STL/TBATS and mark confidence lower

9. Models, diagnostics, and methodology

This section gives Analytics Research full transparency and control.

Primary

X-13ARIMA-SEATS

Use for official-style monthly and quarterly indicators with enough history: CPI, retail, GDP, trade, industrial production.

Flexible

STL decomposition

Use for exploratory analysis, shorter series, and indicators where seasonality evolves gradually.

Advanced

TBATS

Use for complex multi-seasonal patterns in daily or weekly data, such as transactions, mobility, or electricity demand.

12. Governance, audit, and advanced controls

Trust features needed for a powerful SCAD research-grade tool.

Model governance

Every model has a specification version, approval status, analyst owner, methodology notes, and parameter audit trail.

Publication control

Seasonally adjusted outputs inherit raw indicator permissions and can be marked internal, research-only, or publishable.

Quality gates

Residual seasonality failures, unstable factors, or excessive revisions can block publication automatically.

Reproducibility

Outputs are reproducible using the same raw data vintage, calendar table, model specification, and software version.

Bilingual reporting

Template and AI-assisted annotations are generated in English and Arabic with specialist review workflows.

Extra advanced feature

A seasonal-pattern drift score ranks indicators where patterns are changing due to e-commerce, climate, or behaviour shifts.
