8. Complete TNEA Counselling Process

Step 1 — Registration & Documents

  • Portal: tneaonline.org
  • Counselling fee (MBC/DNC): ₹5,000
  • Documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, community certificate, income certificate, FG certificate
  • Verify at TFC

Step 2 — Choice Filling (Round 3)

  • Fill all 50 choices from Section 2 in the same order
  • Select College Code → Branch ECE → confirm
  • Lock before deadline — no edit after lock

Step 3 — Tentative Allotment

  • Based on cutoff, community rank, choice order, seat matrix
  • Check portal with application number

Step 4 — Confirmation (by 5:00 PM)

  • Accept and Join / Accept & Upward / Reject
  • Use Section 7 strategy

Step 5 — Report to College

  • Original documents + fee payment (after FG concession if eligible)
  • Collect admission letter

Step 6 — Upward Movement

  • Only if you chose Accept & Upward
  • If upgraded → report to new college; old seat released

Step 7 — Supplementary Round

  • If no seat in Round 3 — last government-quota chance
